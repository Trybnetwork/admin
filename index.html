Pending Bids

Open High / Low bids placed in the Trade & Save app, waiting on a result. Settlement is by direction, not per person: Reward HIGH and every pending High bid wins — stake back plus an 85% payout to that user's Main Balance — while every pending Low bid loses its stake, which was already locked when the bid was placed. Reward LOW does the reverse. All amounts are virtual.

Deposit & Withdrawal Requests

Approving applies the amount to that user's Main Balance. Rejections leave every balance untouched.

TrybCoin Requests

Users tap Completed on the Earn page after finishing tasks. Enter the coin amount you want to reward (they may have completed only a few tasks), then approve — or decline after checking their proof.

Pending Bids

Open High / Low bids placed in the TrybNetwork Admin app, waiting on a result. Settlement is by direction, not per person: Reward HIGH and every pending High bid wins — stake × 1.9 credited to that user's Main Balance (stake back plus a 90% payout) — while every pending Low bid loses its stake, which was already locked when the bid was placed. Reward LOW does the reverse. For each winning bid, the winner's referrer (if any) also gets 1% of the win profit — (stake × 0.9) × 1% — credited to their Main Balance, once per bid.

Reset Data

Clear history removes only processed deposit/withdrawal requests and settled bids — accounts, balances, and pending items stay untouched. Reset clears all data — registered accounts, balances, requests, referrals, and trade history — for both this panel and the TrybNetwork Admin app on this device.
